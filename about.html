About Kilo Auto Spares

A Nairobi-based supplier of genuine and aftermarket auto parts, built for drivers and workshops who need the right part the first time.

Genuine parts, delivered on time

Every part we list is sourced and verified against the fitment shown on the page — the make, model and year you see is the vehicle it's built for, not an approximation. Where we stock aftermarket alternatives alongside genuine parts, both are clearly labelled, so you can choose based on budget without any guesswork about what you're actually buying.

Once you send an enquiry, we confirm stock and price the same day and agree a firm delivery or pickup window with you upfront — no open-ended "we'll get back to you." For deliveries, we coordinate directly so you know exactly when your part is arriving, and for pickup, your order is set aside and ready the moment you walk in.

Who we are

Kilo Auto Spares Ltd. supplies genuine and aftermarket parts for a wide range of vehicle makes and models on Kenyan roads. Every listing in our shop shows the brand, category, and exact fitment — make, model and year — so you can confirm a part fits before you ever have to ask.

We keep the ordering process simple on purpose: browse or search, build your list, and send it to us on WhatsApp or email. We take it from there — confirming price and stock, and arranging payment and delivery or pickup directly with you.
